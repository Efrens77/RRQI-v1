    What these metrics mean

    Elevation: raster terrain estimate, not phone altitude or precise pavement grade. Open-Meteo uses a ~90 m DEM.

    Road exposure: qualitative proxy calculated from nearby OpenStreetMap road classification, posted speed, and lanes when available. It is not a traffic volume count.

    AADT: actual annual-average daily traffic at a nearby Caltrans counting location, when available. It may refer to a different road and never means current traffic.

    Sources: Open-Meteo / Copernicus DEM · © OpenStreetMap contributors · Caltrans AADT. Mapillary images remain © their respective contributors; see attribution in the viewer.
